{{ section_head('The model', 'Two phases of transfer learning.', 'We started from MobileNetV2 — a network designed for phones — and taught it to read plant disease in two careful steps.') }}
Phase 1 · 94.6%

Train the head

With the pretrained base frozen, we trained only the new classification layer for 10 epochs at a learning rate of 1e-3.

Phase 2 · 97.28%

Fine-tune the top

We unfroze the top 30 layers and trained for 10 more epochs at a learning rate 100× smaller, so the model adapted without forgetting what it knew.

{{ icon('info') }}
A note on the number. 97.28% is measured on PlantVillage validation images, which are cleaner than photos from a field. That’s why every result shows its confidence and AgroSense asks for a second opinion when it isn’t sure. Field data from farmers in Ghana is the next step.
