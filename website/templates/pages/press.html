About AgroSense

AgroSense is an AI crop advisory service for smallholder farmers in Ghana. Farmers photograph a sick leaf and receive a diagnosis, its severity and a treatment plan written for local conditions, alongside weather-based planting advice and market prices from five major markets. The service also works over SMS for farmers without smartphones. AgroSense is based in Accra and is currently in beta.
