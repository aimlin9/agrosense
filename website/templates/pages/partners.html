Weeks 1–2

Plan together

We agree the crops, regions and what success looks like — accuracy, time-to-action, and farmer satisfaction.

Weeks 3–4

Onboard farmers and officers

We train lead farmers and extension officers, set up groups, and enable SMS for members without smartphones.

Weeks 5–10

Run the season

Farmers scan, officers review, and we collect field photos to improve the model for your crops.

Weeks 11–12

Measure and decide

A joint report on outcomes against the goals we set. Then you decide whether to continue.
