{{ section_head('Pricing questions', 'Good to know.') }}
Will farmers ever be charged?

No. Diagnosis, advice, prices and SMS help stay free for individual farmers. Network charges for data or SMS are set by your mobile operator.

What happens after the pilot?

We share a joint report on the goals we agreed. If you continue, we agree a programme plan; if you don’t, your farmers keep their free accounts.

Do you work outside Ghana?

Ghana is our focus during the beta. The model and platform work anywhere; markets, products and languages need local work, which we’re happy to plan with partners in the region.
