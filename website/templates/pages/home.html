01 · CHOOSE

Pick the crop you’re growing

AgroSense only weighs diseases that affect that crop, so there are fewer wrong answers — and it tells you when a crop is still in early access.

02 · PHOTOGRAPH

FAQ

Questions farmers ask us.

More answers in the help centre.

How accurate is it?

The model reached 97.3% accuracy on held-out leaf photos. Field photos are harder than lab images, so every result shows its confidence, and AgroSense asks for a better photo or an extension officer when it isn’t sure.

Does it cost anything?

No. AgroSense is free for farmers. Standard data or SMS charges from your network may apply.

Do I need a smartphone?

No. Prices, weather and help work by SMS on any phone. Photo diagnosis by SMS needs a phone that can send picture messages.

Does it replace my extension officer?

No. It helps you act fast between visits and shows the officer exactly what you saw.
